Saudi Tax Invoice with ZATCA Phase 2 QR

A bilingual Arabic / English tax invoice, simplified tax invoice and credit note report that prints the Phase 2 e-invoicing QR code of the cleared or reported invoice.

⚠️ Where the Phase 2 QR comes from
The Phase 2 QR (invoice hash, ECDSA signature, public key and certificate signature) can only be produced by signing the invoice XML with the CSID issued by ZATCA. Every confirmed invoice prints its Phase 2 QR, whether it has been sent to ZATCA or not: invoices that are not signed yet are signed locally on print by your ZATCA integration module (KSA Zatca Phase-2, ksa_zatca_integration) with your CSID, without sending anything. Once ZATCA clears a standard invoice, the QR of the cleared XML is printed. Companies or periods still in Phase 1 get a Phase 1 QR (tags 1–5).

How it works

1
Post the invoice
Confirm the customer invoice or credit note as usual.
2
ZATCA clears or reports it
Your ZATCA integration signs the XML and sends it; ZATCA accepts it.
3
Print “Tax Invoice (ZATCA)”
The PDF carries the right title and the Phase 2 QR code.

Key features

✔ Tax Invoice or Simplified Tax Invoice
Companies get “فاتورة ضريبية / Tax Invoice”, individuals get “فاتورة ضريبية مبسطة / Simplified Tax Invoice”, matching the ZATCA invoice type.
✔ ZATCA-ready credit notes
“إشعار دائن / Credit Note” with the original invoice number, its date and the reason for issuance.
✔ Phase 2 QR code
Prints the QR of the cleared / reported invoice with all nine tags, embedded in the PDF with no server round-trip.
✔ Every required line detail
Description, quantity, unit price, discount, taxable amount, VAT rate, VAT amount and total including VAT.
✔ VAT in SAR for foreign currency
Invoices in another currency also show the total VAT in the company currency, as ZATCA requires.
✔ Light and safe to install
No stored computed fields, so installing on a large database never recomputes all of your invoices.
Configuration
  1. Install the module (it only depends on Accounting).
  2. Set the company VAT number and address.
  3. For Phase 2, install and onboard your ZATCA integration module (KSA Zatca Phase-2).
  4. Open a customer invoice or credit note and print Tax Invoice (ZATCA).
